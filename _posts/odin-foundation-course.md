The course encourages practical learning using essential tools like IDE (Visual Studio Code), Linux, Terminal, Git, and Github. Throughout the program, you'll delve into extensive documentation, draw your own conclusions, and apply your knowledge by building simple projects."

The Odin Project Foundations was one of my early steps into web development. Its projects made me use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and the terminal directly. This review reflects that stage of learning and the habits I still rely on when building and debugging complete applications.
